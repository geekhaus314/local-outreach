# Layout Blueprint: Jay & Nikki Automotive
**Target Focus:** 5 PM - 9 PM After-Hours Car Care for Working Professionals
**Design Concept:** Dark, high-contrast industrial theme (deep charcoals, electric orange accents) built to load under 400ms on a mobile data connection.

## 1. Hero Section (Above the Fold)
*   **Visual Hook:** High-contrast background with a sharp header. If using an interactive canvas tracer, a subtle amber or neon-orange vector line accent that trace-follows mouse movement or scroll paths.
*   **Bold Headline:** "Car Trouble After Your 9-to-5? We’re Open 5 PM – 9 PM."
*   **Subheadline:** "Florissant’s premier independent auto shop built entirely around the working professional’s schedule. Drop it off after work; pick it up fixed."
*   **Primary CTA Button:** `[ Request an Evening Slot ]` -> Smooth-scroll anchors directly down to a simplified contact fragment.
*   **Trust Anchors:** Mini badges under the button: `⚡ Hardened Security` | `📍 745 N Hwy 67` | `⭐ 5.0 Star Rated`

## 2. The "Friction Killer" Section (3-Column Grid)
*   **Column 1: Hassle-Free Drop Off**
    *   *Copy:* Drop your vehicle off right after your shift ends. No rushing, no missed work, no losing your morning.
*   **Column 2: Straightforward Diagnostics**
    *   *Copy:* Complete transparency on deep engine diagnostics, brakes, and electrical. No mystery fees or surprise phone call bills.
*   **Column 3: Bulletproof Security**
    *   *Copy:* Your appointment data, vehicle records, and personal contact info are stored within a hardened, attack-surface-mapped system architecture.

## 3. Specialized Capabilities Fragment
*   **Automotive Service Stack:** Brakes, suspension, timing chains, heavy engine work, heating/AC repair.
*   **Motorcycle Service Stack:** Specialized track/street bike diagnostic setups, fluid overhauls, mechanical triage.

## 4. Low-Friction Appointment Request (The Payload)
*   **Form Architecture:** Minimal input payload to maximize conversion rate (only 3 fields):
    1. First Name / Phone Number
    2. Vehicle Make/Model
    3. Short description of the issue (e.g., "Squeaking brakes", "Check engine light")
*   **Submit Button:** `[ Secure My Slot ]`
*   **Post-Submit Event:** Hooks straight into your HubSpot CRM lead capture workflow to trigger a rapid text follow-up.
